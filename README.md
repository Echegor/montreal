## Route Architecture
* **Phase I: Hudson River Twilight & Gilded Age (Thu Oct 29):** Newark departure via Palisades Parkway & Mario Cuomo Bridge to Saratoga Springs (*The Adelphi Hotel* / Woodlawn Garage). Late-night craft cocktails and smash burgers at *Hamlet & Ghost*.
* **Phase II: High Peaks & French-Canadian River Spa (Fri Oct 30):** Mrs. London's almond croissants, I-87 Northway rock cuts, Poke-O-Moonshine fire tower hike (2,180 ft), Samuel De Champlain bridge skyline reveal, check-in at *Hôtel William Gray* in Old Montreal, evening hydrotherapy at *Bota Bota, spa-sur-l'eau*, and midnight French bistro supper at *L'Express*.
* **Phase III: Montreal Culture to Lake Champlain Islands (Sat Oct 31):** Fairmount hot sesame bagels, Plateau spiral staircases, Mount Royal panorama, Portuguese poutine at *Ma Poule Mouillée*, Highgate Springs border crossing, and US-2 sandbar water drive to *Hotel Vermont* in Burlington. Sunset at Waterfront Park and hearthside dinner at *Hen of the Wood*.
* **Phase IV: Green Mountain Foothills & Parkway Cruise (Sun Nov 1):** August First cardamom buns, Vermont Route 7 South through Middlebury and Manchester, and commercial-free cruising down the Taconic State Parkway back into Newark.
