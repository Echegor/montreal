INTERNATIONAL BORDER PROTOCOL

Customs & Border Protection Checklist

  • Documents in Cabin: Keep physical passports within arm's reach in the center console. Do not pack them in the trunk.
  • Friday Entry (Lacolle): Cross at St-Bernard-de-Lacolle via I-87 North onto Autoroute 15 Nord at ~1:30 PM. Expect 5–15 min wait.
  • Saturday Return (Highgate Springs): Exit Quebec via Route 133 to US I-89 South at ~2:45 PM. Virtually zero queues.
  • Declarations: Declare any packaged food. Citrus fruits, firewood, and cannabis are strictly prohibited from crossing in either direction.
DATA ROAMING: Toggle "Data Roaming" to ON before reaching the border. AT&T, Verizon, and T-Mobile seamlessly hand off to Rogers/Bell without extra setup on unlimited North American plans.
FINANCIAL & TOLL SETUP

Tolls, Currency & Parking

  • NY E-ZPass: Mounted in windshield; covers Gov. Mario M. Cuomo Bridge and all I-87 Thruway tolls seamlessly.
  • Zero-Fee Credit Cards: Montreal and Burlington are completely cashless. Set a 0% foreign transaction fee credit card as default in Apple Wallet / Google Pay.
  • Montreal Parking Hack: Park at the Chaussegros-de-Léry heated underground garage (330 Rue Champ-de-Mars) or use hotel valet at William Gray.

NEIGHBORHOOD ARCHITECTURE

North Broadway Gilded Age Mansions

Before hitting the highway Friday morning, take a 5-minute slow drive down North Broadway (between Van Dam St and Skidmore College).

This grand boulevard preserves one of the most remarkable collections of Queen Anne, Greek Revival, and High Victorian Gothic mansions in North America, once owned by Gilded Age tycoons and racing barons.

The Adirondack Northway: Lakes, Gorges & Peaks

Morning croissants at Mrs. London's followed by one of America's most scenic designated wilderness interstates.

Mrs. London’s Bakery (464 Broadway)

Legendary French boulangerie operating in Saratoga since 1977.

DEPARTURE CALL: Grab breakfast at 8:00 AM and hit I-87 North by 8:45 AM to time the hike perfectly before border crossing.
NORTHWAY HIGHWAY VISTAS

Key Vantage Points Along I-87 North

  • Mile Marker 67–72 (Exit 24): I-87 carves through exposed granite bedrock with views of the Tongue Mountain Range.
  • Exit 28 Rest Area Overlook: Quick 3-minute pull-off behind the rest area building for elevated views of Schroon Lake reflecting autumn ridgelines.
  • Mile Marker 92–96 (High Peaks Overlook): The most dramatic mountain reveal on the interstate—the 4,627-ft peak of Giant Mountain looms directly over the highway.

THE SUMMIT PANORAMA

Restored 1917 Aeromotor Fire Tower

Step onto the massive anorthosite granite summit ledges:

  • Looking East: The vast expanse of Lake Champlain with Vermont's snow-dusted Green Mountains (Mount Mansfield and Camel's Hump) forming the horizon.
  • Looking Southwest: The entire jagged rim of the Adirondack High Peaks.
  • Timing: 10:15 AM start ➔ 11:30 AM summit ➔ 12:45 PM back at vehicle.
DAY 2 URBAN ARRIVAL • PONT SAMUEL-DE CHAMPLAIN

The Grand Montreal Bridge Reveal

HIGHWAY NAVIGATION

The Center-Left Lane Maneuver

After clearing customs at St-Bernard-de-Lacolle at 1:30 PM, cruise north on Autoroute 15 Nord through the Montérégie plain.

  • The Monoliths: Watch for Mont Saint-Grégoire and Mont Saint-Hilaire rising abruptly from the flat farmland on your right.
  • The Approach: Follow signs for Pont Samuel-De Champlain. Stay in the center-left lane.
  • The Money Shot: Cresting the bridge, downtown Montreal's glass skyscraper canyon frames directly ahead with the green summit of Mount Royal rising behind it.
HOTEL DROP-IN

Express Connection into Old Montreal

Immediately upon touching the island of Montreal, take the exit for Route 136 Est (Ville-Marie Expressway) toward Centre-Ville / Vieux-Montréal.

  • Take the Rue Saint-Antoine / Rue Saint-Jacques exit.
  • Drop directly south 4 blocks into the historic cobblestone quarter to check in at 3:00 PM with zero gridlock.

DAY 2 EVENING EXPERIENCE • THERMAL CIRCUIT & BISTRO

Bota Bota River Spa & L’Express Bistro

Floating thermal baths under illuminated grain silos followed by Montreal's definitive midnight Parisian supper.

THERMAL HYDROTHERAPY (06:00 PM – 09:00 PM)

Bota Bota, spa-sur-l’eau

A converted historical ferryboat permanently moored in the Old Port of Montreal.

  • The Nordic Circuit: Hot eucalyptus steam baths and dry saunas ➔ ice-cold plunge pools ➔ outdoor whirlpools overlooking the St. Lawrence.
  • The Visual: As twilight fades, steam rises into the cold autumn air with illuminated views of the historic port, Silo 5, and Habitat 67.
  • Protocol: Robe and towel provided; bring swimsuit and clean slide sandals.
MIDNIGHT SUPPER (10:30 PM)

L’Express (3922 Rue Saint-Denis)

Montreal's timeless French bistro with black-and-white checkered floors and zinc bar.

  • Must Order: Os à Moelle (roasted bone marrow with coarse grey sea salt & grilled baguette).
  • Tabletop Ritual: A massive jar of French cornichons and Dijon mustard placed on every table.
  • Main: Classic Steak Tartare with frites or Rillettes du Mans, paired with a glass of Gamay or Côtes du Rhône.
DAY 3 MORNING • SATURDAY, OCTOBER 31, 2026

Fairmount Bagels & Plateau Staircases

THE BAGEL RITE (08:30 AM)

Fairmount Bagel (74 Ave Fairmount Ouest)

Operating 24 hours a day since 1919. Boiled in honey water and baked in a cavernous wood-fired oven.

  • Insider Order: Half-dozen hot sesame bagels straight off the wooden peel.
  • How to Eat: Skip the cream cheese tub inside; tear into one piping-hot bagel right out of the brown paper bag on the sidewalk. Chewy, slightly sweet, with wood smoke crust.

DAY 3 LUNCH • 969 RUE RACHEL EST

Ma Poule Mouillée: Portuguese Poutine

Charcoal-grilled Portuguese chicken and spicy chouriço meet Quebec's iconic national comfort dish.

THE LEGENDARY ORDER

Poutine Portugaise

Widely ranked by local chefs and food critics as the greatest poutine variant in Montreal.

  • The Composition: Fresh hand-cut fries, charcoal-rotisserie shredded chicken, thick slices of grilled spicy Portuguese chouriço, traditional squeaky cheese curds PLUS shaved São Jorge cheese, drenched in house piri-piri chicken gravy.
  • Size Recommendation: Order a Regular (plenty to share) or two Smalls.
AL FRESCO PICNIC STRATEGY

Parc La Fontaine

Rather than fighting for crowded indoor diner tables, walk across Rue Rachel directly into Parc La Fontaine.

  • Claim a park bench beside the central pond.
  • Eat your poutine under weeping willow trees watching cyclists and ducks before walking back to retrieve your car.
DAY 3 SCENIC DETOUR • US ROUTE 2 SOUTH

The Lake Champlain Islands Water Drive

Crossing the Vermont border at Highgate Springs and driving directly across open-water sandbar causeways.

THE ROUTE TO VERMONT

Highgate Springs Border Crossing (02:45 PM)

Depart Montreal at 1:45 PM via Autoroute 10 Est to Autoroute 35 Sud toward the Vermont border.

  • Quiet Border Crossing: Highgate Springs (I-89 South) is fast and uncongested compared to New York crossings.
  • Switch to US-2 West: At Swanton, exit onto US Route 2 toward the Champlain Islands (Alburgh, North Hero, Grand Isle, South Hero).
THE CAUSEWAY EXPERIENCE

Driving Across Lake Champlain

US Route 2 carries you across thin ribbons of causeways with open water lapping on both sides of the car.

  • Dual Mountain Horizons: To your right, the jagged skyline of New York's Adirondacks; to your left, Vermont's Green Mountains.
  • Sand Bar State Park Causeway: The final bridge crossing into Colchester, driving directly at water level before arriving in Burlington by 4:30 PM.
